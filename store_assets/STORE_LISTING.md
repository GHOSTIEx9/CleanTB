## 2. Long Description

```markdown
Transform your Windows 11 and Windows 10 desktop with CleanTB — the ultra-lightweight, bloat-free taskbar customizer featuring an integrated real-time Hardware HUD.

Unlike heavy customization suites bundled with sluggish UI frameworks and telemetry tracking, CleanTB is built from the ground up using native Win32 APIs and direct Windows Shell integration. It delivers crystal-clear transparency and essential performance stats while sipping just ~20–30 MB of RAM.

✨ KEY FEATURES:
• Seamless Taskbar Transparency: Choose from Clear (100% transparent), Fluent Acrylic Blur, Frosted Glass, or subtle Custom Tint styles.
• Live Hardware HUD Stats: Monitor real-time Network Download/Upload speeds, CPU load, RAM usage, and GPU 3D engine metrics pinned directly on your taskbar.
• Zero Jitter Layout: Numbers and stats transition smoothly with fixed-width formatting and anti-jitter anchoring.
• Fullscreen & Maximize Aware: The hardware stats HUD stays pinned neatly to your taskbar and automatically hides when full-screen applications, games, or media players are active.
• Multi-Monitor Support: Clean styling across your primary display and all connected secondary monitor taskbars.
• Ultra-Low Resource Usage: Pure native Win32 message loop maintaining ~20–30 MB RAM footprint with virtually zero CPU overhead (< 0.2%).
• 100% Offline & Private: Zero telemetry, zero analytics, zero external network calls. Your hardware data never leaves your device.
• Instant 1-Click Quick Toggle: Left-click the system tray icon to switch between transparent and default Windows taskbars instantly.
• Windows 11 & 10 Native: Full support for Explorer restart recovery and display change events.

Take back your screen real estate and keep an eye on your system health with CleanTB!
```
